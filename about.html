Our story

We Are Kharis

Faith, purpose, and community in action.

Knowing God. Doing exploits.

A ministry shaped by grace and sent with purpose.

Kauga Kharis Word International Ministries is a faith-based organization founded in 2018 by Apostle Etindi Moses. We exist to raise people who know God, serve others, and live with purpose.

Why we exist

Our Objective

To advance the Kingdom of God through evangelism, education, health, youth and women empowerment, and community development.

Our Mission

To preach the Word of Grace and empower communities spiritually, socially, and economically through faith-driven programs.

Where we are going

Our Vision

To raise a strong and God-knowing people who will do exploits in every area of life.

Our Core Values

  • FaithTrusting God’s Word and promises.
  • LoveShowing God’s love through service.
  • IntegrityLiving honestly and accountably.
  • ExcellenceServing God with care and high standards.
  • UnityPromoting oneness in Christ.
  • ServiceUsing our gifts to help others.
