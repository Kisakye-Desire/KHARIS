Kauga Kharis Word international ministries is a faith-based organization founded in 2018 by Apostle Etindi Moses, a medical doctor by profession with a Master’s in Public Health. He also holds a Certificate in Christian Leadership and Theology. The ministry began as a Thursday evening fellowship at Mukono Resort Hotel and later expanded to full Sunday services following the Covid-19 pandemic.

Our Objective.

To advance the Kingdom of God through evangelism, education, health, youth and women empowerment, and community development programs.

Core Values

  • Faith: Trust in God’s Word and promises.
  • Love: Showing God’s love through service.
  • Integrity: Living with honesty and accountability.
  • Excellence: Serving God with high standards.
  • Unity: Promoting oneness in Christ.
  • Service: Using our gifts to help others.
  • Discipleship: Growing believers in Christ.

Our Mission.

To preach the Word of Grace and empower
communities, spiritually, socially
and economically through faith driven programs

Our Vision

To raise a strong and God- knowing people who will do exploits in every areas of life.


What we do

Evangelism and Discipleship

Evangelism and Discipleship reaching out to the soul with the gospel and nurturing them in truth.

Youth and Children ministry

Youth and Children ministry Training the next generation in God's word and leadership.

Women Empowerment

Women Empowerment Equipping women to rise in Faith, Family and community.

Education and Empowerment

Education and skill development : Providing learning opportunities that build hope.
