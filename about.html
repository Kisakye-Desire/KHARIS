Who we are

We are Kharis

Kharis Word Ministries is a faith-based ministry founded in 2018 by Apostle Etindi Moses, a medical doctor with a Master’s in Public Health and training in Christian leadership.

Our mission

Grace that empowers

To preach the Word of Grace and empower communities spiritually, socially and economically through faith-driven programmes.

Our vision

A people who do exploits

To raise a strong and God-knowing people who will do exploits in every area of life.

Our values

Faith & love

Trusting God’s Word and making His love visible through service.

Integrity & excellence

Serving with honesty, accountability and our very best.

Unity & service

Growing together and using our gifts to help others.

Discipleship

Building believers who live the Word with courage and compassion.
