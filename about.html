Our story and heart

We Are Kharis

A welcoming church family learning to know God and do exploits.

Knowing God. Doing exploits.

A ministry shaped by grace and sent with purpose.

Kauga Kharis Word International Ministries is a faith-based organization founded in 2018 by Apostle Etindi Moses. We exist to raise people who know God, serve others, and live with purpose.

Our foundation

From a fellowship to a family

The ministry began as a Thursday evening fellowship at Mukono Resort Hotel and later expanded into full Sunday services after the Covid-19 pandemic. Our story is still being written through every life changed, prayer answered, and family welcomed.

We believe church is more than a weekly meeting. It is a family where people can ask honest questions, receive care, discover their gifts, and grow into the calling God has placed within them.

Our Mission

To preach the Word of Grace and empower communities spiritually, socially, and economically through faith-driven programs.

Our Vision

To raise a strong and God-knowing people who will do exploits in every area of life.
