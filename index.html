Our story

A place to know God and live with purpose.

Founded in 2018 by Apostle Etindi Moses, Kharis began as a Thursday evening fellowship at Mukono Resort Hotel. Today, we are a growing family committed to the Word of Grace and the transformation of lives.

Whether you are exploring faith, returning to church, or looking for a place to serve, there is room for you here.
