Contact Us

We would love to hear from you and connect with you. Kauga Kharis Word international ministries is located in Kauga, Mukono, near Mukono Prison, and we warmly welcome you to visit us for fellowship, prayer, and the teaching of God’s Word.

Whether you have a prayer request, need counseling, want to partner with the ministry, or require more information about our services and programs, please feel free to reach out to us. Our team is ready to respond and walk with you in faith. You can contact us through phone, email, or by visiting our church premises, and we look forward to hearing from you and serving you.
